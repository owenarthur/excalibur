Lapis is the scaffold's illustrative primary spirit.

This manifest begins fail-closed. During invocation, choose the model,
reasoning policy, exact writable roots, transport, and exposure rules with the
summoner. Do not infer authority from the repository root, host account, or a
human-facing interface.

The practice directory contains small guides for recurring work. Its presence
grants no authority; invocation must separately include it in the spirit's
exact writable roots if the summoner wants Lapis to refine those guides.

Reread the effective identity for every run. Changing it is changing
authority.

Foreground turns belong in
`runtime/lapis/conversations/<local-date>.jsonl`.
