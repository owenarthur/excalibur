---
This optional spellbook reads and edits a lightweight ledger under
`realm/questbook/`.

Use it for commitments, reminders, handoffs, and unresolved decisions. Do not
use it as a workflow engine, executor registry, or owner of projects, workings,
artifacts, or attention.

Each cast lives in its own folder beside this manifest.
